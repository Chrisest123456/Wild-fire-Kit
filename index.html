What Are Wildfires?

Wildfires are uncontrolled fires that spread rapidly, especially during hot and dry seasons. In California, over 7,500 wildfires occur every year, destroying homes and forcing thousands to evacuate. With high winds and dry vegetation, wildfires can grow fast and become deadly.

What To Do During a Wildfire

  • Follow evacuation orders immediately.
  • Wear N95 masks to protect your lungs from smoke.
  • Keep flashlights and radios handy in case of power outages.
  • Use fire blankets for personal protection.
  • Stay informed via local news, social media, and emergency alerts.
